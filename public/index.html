How it works

A creator-first publishing flow.

Zoe Studio keeps the important decisions in the creator's hands. Posting controls are refreshed for the connected account before publication rather than assumed or preselected.

STEP 01

Connect securely

Sign in through TikTok's authorization flow. Sensitive access credentials stay on the server side and are not exposed to browser JavaScript.

STEP 02

Prepare original content

Select a video or photos, write the post text, and review the account's current privacy and interaction options.

STEP 03

Approve each post

No privacy level is preselected and interaction controls start off. A publication is initiated only after the creator explicitly confirms it.

Designed for clarity

Know what will be sent.

  • Current creator-specific privacy options are loaded before posting.
  • Comments, Duet, and Stitch controls respect account capabilities.
  • Posting requires an explicit consent confirmation.
  • Video transfer is streamed to TikTok after initialization.
  • Temporary photo media is automatically expired from Zoe Studio storage.
Your choices

Disconnect when you want.

Zoe Studio uses authorization only for the publishing features the creator chooses to use. The app does not expose access or refresh tokens in the browser.
