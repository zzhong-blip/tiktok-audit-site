Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zoe Studio handles information when creators use the web application to prepare and publish original video and photo content.

1. Information we process

  • Authorization information: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued after you authorize Zoe Studio, along with the scopes you grant and token expiration information.
  • Creator information: information returned by the connected platform for publishing, such as creator nickname, avatar, available privacy choices, interaction capabilities, and maximum posting duration where provided.
  • Post information: titles, descriptions, privacy selections, interaction choices, commercial-content toggles, AI-generated-content disclosure choices, media metadata, and publication status identifiers needed to perform the requested posting action.
  • Media: video data is transferred for the requested publication. Photos may be stored temporarily on Zoe Studio infrastructure so that the connected platform can retrieve them from a verified URL.
  • Technical information: standard service logs may include timestamps, request paths, error information, and security-related metadata needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How we use information

We process this information to authenticate your session, display the posting controls available to your connected creator account, initialize and transfer content you explicitly choose to publish, show publication status, maintain security, diagnose errors, and comply with applicable platform requirements.

3. OAuth token security

Zoe Studio keeps access and refresh tokens in encrypted, secure, HTTP-only session storage. Tokens are not exposed to client-side JavaScript. Server-side secrets are not embedded in public web pages or source delivered to the browser.

4. Media retention

Videos are streamed for the requested transfer and are not intentionally retained by Zoe Studio after the transfer. Photos uploaded for URL-based publishing are stored under randomized URLs and are automatically scheduled for deletion within approximately 24 hours. Platform copies are governed by the connected platform's policies.

5. Sharing

We send information to the connected platform only as necessary to perform the action you request. We do not sell personal information. We do not provide OAuth access or refresh tokens to advertisers.

6. Cookies

Zoe Studio uses security and session cookies to protect the OAuth flow, prevent request forgery, and maintain an authenticated creator session. These cookies are used for service operation rather than advertising.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to connect an account, decline requested permissions, avoid publishing, or disconnect the Zoe Studio session. You can also revoke app authorization through the connected platform's account controls.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, server-side secret handling, secure and HTTP-only cookies, origin checks, input validation, constrained media routes, and short-lived temporary media storage. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Zoe Studio is not directed to children who are not eligible to use the connected creator platform or grant the relevant authorization under applicable rules.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, platform APIs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

11. Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the support channel identified in the Zoe Studio application or its official project documentation.
